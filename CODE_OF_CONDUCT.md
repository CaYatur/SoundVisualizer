# Code of conduct

This project is a place to build and talk about a music visualizer. Everyone who takes part in the
issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to keep it pleasant to work in.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not with the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Keep feedback specific and about the work: what happens, what you expected, how to reproduce it.
- Write in English or Turkish; both are welcome here.
- Remember that maintainers and contributors are volunteers.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, harassment or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, advertising, or repeatedly pushing a topic after a decision has been made.

## Scope

This applies to every space of the project: the repository, its issues, pull requests and
discussions, and anywhere someone represents the project.

## Reporting

If something is wrong, write to **contact@cayadev.com**. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept
confidential. For a security problem use the [security policy](SECURITY.md) instead.

## What happens next

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break
these rules, and may block someone who keeps breaking them, temporarily or permanently. Decisions
are explained to the people involved.
